Zororo Phumulani — Full Policy Terms & Conditions

General

  1. Premiums due monthly in advance by the 1st of each month.
  2. Main member age 18–65 to join. Maximum entry age 65 (next birthday).
  3. Insured lives limited to those declared on the application form.
  4. Extended family cover: residents of SA or Zimbabwe only. Max age 90. Cash claimable if deceased is outside SA/ZIM.
  5. Cohabiting couples qualify for family benefits if declared on form.
  6. Max 6 unmarried children under 21. Extended to 26 if full-time student. Physically/mentally disabled dependants covered (no grant, dependent on parents).

Waiting Periods

  1. Cover commences on the 1st day of the month only.
  2. Accidental death: immediate cover from first premium.
  3. Natural causes — immediate family: 3 calendar months.
  4. Natural causes — extended family: 6 calendar months.
  5. Suicide: 12 calendar months.
  6. Newborns enjoy immediate cover if added within 6 months of birth.

Exclusions

  • Nuclear, biological or chemical weapons or radioactive contamination.
  • Sabotage of facilities releasing radioactive or biochemical agents.
  • Involvement of insured lives in unlawful activity.
  • Wilful self-injury or influence of alcohol/narcotics/drugs (unless prescribed by registered doctor).

Premiums & Claims

  1. Month-to-month basis. No surrender value. Premiums payable lifelong.
  2. Claims must be submitted within 6 months of the date of death.
  3. Policy lapses after 3 months of non-payment.
  4. 60-day grace period for arrears before policy is cancelled.
  5. Foster children excluded unless proof of legal adoption supplied.

Children Benefit Scale

14+ years: 100%  |  6–13 years: 50%  |  0–5 years: 25%  |  Stillborn (28+ weeks): 25%

Claim Documents Required

  1. Main member ID/Passport (certified copy).
  2. Deceased ID/Passport or Registrar General affidavit.
  3. Certified death certificate or signed burial order.
  4. SAPS accident report if cause of death is unnatural.
  5. Doctor's letter confirming pregnancy months (for stillbirth).
  6. Notice of death if the person passed away in South Africa.
  7. Mother's certified ID/Passport in case of a minor or stillbirth.

Termination

  • 1 month written notice of cancellation by either party.
  • Policy lapses on non-payment or withdrawal from the scheme.
  • Cover ceases immediately on policyholder withdrawal.
